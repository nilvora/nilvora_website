Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Nilvora handles personal information when you visit our website, contact us, or use Nilvora’s booking and marketplace services. We aim to collect only information reasonably needed to respond to enquiries, arrange services, process transactions and protect the platform.

1. Information we may collect

  • Contact details: such as your name, email address and phone number when you contact us or make a booking request.
  • Booking and order details: such as selected service or product, dates, quantities, guest or delivery details, and communications needed to fulfil the request.
  • Payment status: payment confirmations, transaction references and refund status supplied by our payment provider. We do not intend to collect or store full payment-card numbers or security codes on this website.
  • Technical information: limited device, browser, log or usage information that may be generated when the website is accessed. Any analytics or cookies beyond essential site functions should be disclosed if enabled.

2. How we use information

We may use personal information to respond to enquiries, provide booking or order support, communicate confirmations and updates, coordinate with relevant service providers, process payments or refunds through payment partners, prevent fraud and misuse, meet legal obligations, and improve the service.

3. Sharing information

We may share the minimum necessary information with a relevant boat operator, accommodation, activity or dining provider, marketplace seller, delivery partner, payment processor, technical service provider, or public authority where required by law. We do not sell personal information as a business practice.

4. Payments and external services

Online payments, when available, may be processed by a third-party payment service. That provider’s own privacy and security terms may also apply. Links to external websites are governed by those websites’ policies, not this one.

5. Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including customer support, accounting, dispute handling and legal compliance. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with your enquiry or to request correction or deletion where applicable. We may need to retain certain records to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law.

7. Children

Our services are intended to be arranged by adults. Please do not send children’s personal information unless it is necessary for a booking and you are authorized to provide it.

8. Cookies and updates

The website may use essential browser storage or similar technologies needed for site functionality. If we add analytics, advertising or other non-essential tracking tools, this policy should be updated and any required consent obtained. We may update this policy by publishing a new version here.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at the email or phone number below. Please do not send payment-card details or sensitive identity documents through ordinary email.
