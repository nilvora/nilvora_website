Cancellation & Refund Policy

Draft for review: This page is not a promise of a specific refund outcome. Final rules must be defined before accepting bookings or product payments and must comply with applicable law and provider requirements.

Nilvora is preparing a platform for experiences, stays, dining and local marketplace products in Aswan. This website does not currently confirm or process purchases.

Before confirming a future purchase

Before a customer pays, the applicable provider, service or product details, total price, delivery or experience timing, cancellation window and refund conditions should be displayed clearly.

Cancellation requests

When booking and purchasing functionality becomes available, customers will be given an official channel for submitting cancellation or change requests. The applicable conditions may differ between accommodation, boat trips, dining, day-use experiences and physical products; the conditions should be disclosed before confirmation.

Refund processing

Where a refund is approved, the method and expected processing time should be communicated to the customer. Actual posting times may depend on the payment provider and the customer's bank. Nilvora should not promise a fixed refund timeline until the payment-provider process has been confirmed.

Contact

For questions about planned services, contact hello@nilvora.online. Confirm that this address is monitored and replace this draft with your final operational policy before processing transactions.
