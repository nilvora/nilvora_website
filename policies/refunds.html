Cancellation & Refund Policy

This policy explains how cancellation and refund requests are handled for bookings and marketplace orders made through Nilvora. The exact terms can depend on the service or product, the provider, the timing of the request and applicable law. Any specific cancellation conditions and deposit terms should be shown to the customer before payment.

1. Before you pay

Please review the service or product description, date, quantity, total price, delivery or attendance details, deposit amount (if any), and the cancellation terms shown for that transaction. If any important detail is unclear, contact Nilvora before paying.

2. Booking cancellations

For boats, stays, activities, dining and day-use bookings, cancellation eligibility and any applicable fee depend on the conditions communicated for that specific booking. To request cancellation, contact Nilvora as soon as possible and include your booking reference, name and the reason for the request. We will review the applicable terms and confirm the outcome.

3. Deposits and no-shows

A deposit is refundable or non-refundable only according to the deposit and cancellation terms disclosed before payment, subject to applicable law. If you do not arrive or use the service at the agreed time, a refund may not be available where the disclosed terms lawfully provide for this. If the provider cancels or cannot deliver the confirmed service, contact us so we can review an appropriate remedy, which may include a refund or rescheduling where available.

4. Marketplace products

If a product arrives damaged, incorrect or materially different from its description, contact us promptly after delivery and provide your order reference and, where useful, photographs of the item and packaging. We will review the issue with the relevant seller and arrange an appropriate solution in line with the circumstances and applicable law. Change-of-mind returns depend on the product, the terms shown before purchase and any legal rights that apply.

5. Refund approval and processing

Once a refund is approved, it will normally be sent back through the original payment method where supported by the payment provider. The time it takes for the funds to appear depends on the payment provider and the customer’s bank. We will communicate any relevant status or information available to us. A refund may not appear immediately in your account.

6. How to request help

Email nilvora01@gmail.com or call +20 111 068 7890. Include your order or booking reference, transaction reference if available, and a short description of the issue. Please do not send full card numbers or security codes.

7. Consumer rights

This policy does not remove or restrict any mandatory rights you may have under applicable consumer-protection or other laws. If a term in this policy conflicts with a mandatory legal requirement, the legal requirement will apply.
